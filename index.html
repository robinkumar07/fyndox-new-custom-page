About Fyndox

Access to qualified medicine — reimagined.

Fyndox is a digital health platform that connects international patients with qualified doctors. We combine modern technology, multilingual communication and a patient-centred approach to make high-quality healthcare accessible across borders.

The reality

Quality healthcare is not equally accessible to everyone.

Language, distance and bureaucracy separate patients from the care they deserve.

This needs to change.

Our answer

Direct. Discreet. Digital.

No detours. No intermediaries.

Smart matching in any language.

For everyone who deserves better.

Patients

Access top medical specialists from anywhere in the world – in your language, with personal support and a seamless digital experience.

Doctors & Clinics

Reach international patients, provide care across borders and focus on what matters most – medicine.

A broken path to care.

Too many patients face unnecessary obstacles on their way to the right treatment. Fyndox offers a better, simpler and more human way.

The problem

Language barriers

Important medical details can get lost in translation.

Intermediaries

Agencies and middlemen make the process complex and expensive.

Medical document sharing

Sending records is often slow, insecure and unclear.

Follow-up care

Continued support across borders is difficult to organise.

The solution

Smart Matching

Find the right specialist based on your medical needs and language.

Direct access

No intermediaries. Communicate directly with qualified doctors and clinics.

Encrypted, EU-hosted documents

Share your medical records securely and easily.

Video consultations and aftercare

Get expert medical advice and continuous support – from anywhere.

No brokers. No barriers. Just care.

Modern technology.
Human healthcare.
Across borders.
